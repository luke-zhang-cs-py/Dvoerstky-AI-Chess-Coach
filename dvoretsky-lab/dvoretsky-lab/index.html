How the numbers are made

Measured strength blends three things: a FIDE-style performance rating over the last ninety days, a strength estimate derived from average centipawn loss, and your listed rating as an anchor. The blend leans on results once there are enough of them and on move quality when there are not. The margin shown is the sampling error on the performance rating, so it narrows as you play. Motif labels are produced by geometric rules over the position rather than by a neural classifier: they are reliable for forks, pins, hanging pieces and back-rank patterns, and rougher for deflection and prophylaxis.
